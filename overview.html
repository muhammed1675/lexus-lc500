Overview

The Art of Performance

The Lexus LC 500 represents the pinnacle of Lexus design and engineering. A luxury grand tourer that challenges conventions and rewards the senses.

Build Your LC 500 Explore Design
Design Philosophy

Form Follows Emotion

Every curve, every line, every surface has been sculpted with purpose. The LC 500 is a testament to the belief that a car can be both a work of art and a precision instrument.

Flowing Silhouette

The sweeping roofline flows seamlessly from the aggressive front end to the muscular rear haunches, creating a sense of motion even at rest.

Spindle Grille

The signature Lexus spindle grille takes center stage, reimagined with a mesh pattern that suggests both sophistication and aggression.

Sculpted Rear

The rear features available LED taillights and dual exhaust outlets that emphasize the car's wide, planted stance.

Interior

Crafted for the Driver

Step inside and discover an environment where every control falls naturally to hand, where luxury materials surround you, and where driving becomes an immersive experience.

Driver-Centric Cockpit

Controls angled toward the driver for intuitive access

Hand-Stitched Leather

Semi-aniline leather with precision Takumi craftsmanship

Mark Levinson Audio

13-speaker, 915-watt premium surround sound

Heritage

From Vision to Reality

The LC 500 began as the LF-LC concept, unveiled at the 2012 Detroit Auto Show. The overwhelming response from enthusiasts and critics alike led Lexus to do something remarkable: bring the concept to life with almost no compromise to the original design.
